# Privacy Policy for focus-black

**Last Updated: January 11, 2026**

## Overview

focus-black ("we", "our", or "us") operates the focus-black mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

## Information Collection and Use

### Information We DO NOT Collect

focus-black is designed with privacy in mind. We DO NOT:
- Collect personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number)
- Track your location
- Access your contacts or photos
- Share your data with third parties
- Use advertising networks
- Collect browsing history

### Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and NEVER leaves your device:

1. **Wallpaper Preferences**
   - Current wallpaper index
   - Auto-rotation settings
   - Wallpaper apply target (home/lock screen preference)
   - Battery optimization settings

2. **Usage Statistics**
   - Streak count (consecutive days of wallpaper application)
   - Last wallpaper apply timestamp
   - Review gate status (whether in-app review was shown)

3. **Cached Data**
   - Downloaded wallpaper images (if using remote packs)
   - Wallpaper thumbnails

All data is stored using Android SharedPreferences and local cache, accessible only to the app on your device.

## Permissions

The app requires the following Android permissions:

### SET_WALLPAPER
- **Purpose**: To change your device wallpaper
- **Usage**: Only activated when you tap "Apply Wallpaper" or enable auto-rotation

### INTERNET (if using remote packs)
- **Purpose**: To download wallpaper packs from our server
- **Usage**: Only used when loading remote wallpaper content
- **Note**: No personal data is transmitted during these requests

### WAKE_LOCK
- **Purpose**: To ensure background wallpaper rotation completes
- **Usage**: Used by Android WorkManager for scheduled tasks

## Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Data persists until you uninstall the app
- Clearing app data will delete all preferences and cached wallpapers
- We cannot recover your data as we never have access to it

## Third-Party Services

The app does NOT use third-party services such as:
- Analytics platforms (Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, etc.)
- Crash reporting services
- Advertising networks
- Social media SDKs

**Note**: The app uses Google Play In-App Review API, which is a standard Android framework component and does not collect personal information.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is rated for all ages and does not contain:
- User-generated content
- Social features
- External links (except Settings → About)
- In-app purchases (in the free version)
